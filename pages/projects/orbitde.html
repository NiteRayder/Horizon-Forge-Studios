SILVERLINE NETWORK / SYSTEMS

Concept

Orbit

A lightweight, modular desktop environment concept exploring a modern desktop experience for Linux systems with limited resources.

Origin

Orbit began as an exploration of desktop usability in constrained and virtualized Linux environments, including ChromeOS Linux containers. The goal is to investigate an interface that feels modern without requiring the resources of a full desktop stack.

Design goals

Lightweight

Keep resource usage and background services under control.

Modular

Explore replaceable components and a structure that can grow gradually.

Usable

Build a clear interface suited to laptops, touch devices, and everyday workflows.

Current status

Orbit remains a concept and research project. A mature prototype and release timeline have not been established.
