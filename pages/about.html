ABOUT THE STUDIO

Independent by nature.
Intentional by design.

Horizon Forge Studios is an independent software studio exploring practical software, community infrastructure, games, and experimental technology.

What we do

Software and tools

Applications and utilities designed to solve real problems and make digital work easier.

Community systems

Bot and dashboard experiences that help online communities manage their spaces and workflows.

Games and experiments

Game projects and technical explorations that give new ideas room to develop.

The Silverline Network

Silverline Network is the umbrella collection for projects developed across Horizon Forge Studios. Each project has its own purpose, while sharing a consistent approach to usability, maintainability, and thoughtful design.

How we work

Projects move at different speeds. Some are under active development, while others remain concepts or are paused until the right resources are available. We aim to communicate those distinctions clearly rather than presenting plans as finished features.
